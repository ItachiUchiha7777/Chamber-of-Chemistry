Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our pedagogy, batch structures and learning approach.

At COC, teaching is led directly by an M.Sc. and Ph.D. Research Scholar rather than inexperienced temporary staff. We emphasise first-principles conceptual logic rather than superficial formula memorisation. We keep our batches focused, ensuring individual student monitoring and patient doubt-clearing.
Doubt clearance is an integral part of our teaching philosophy. Students can raise queries during live sessions, submit questions in dedicated review sessions, and participate in structured PYQ troubleshooting workshops where common errors are broken down step-by-step.
For NEET and JEE Main Inorganic and Organic Chemistry, NCERT is the non-negotiable benchmark. However, simply reading NCERT is inadequate—students must understand the underlying physical and thermodynamic rationale behind exceptions and trends. For Physical Chemistry, NCERT concepts must be supplemented with rigorous numerical problem-solving drills, which COC provides in structured worksheets.
To maintain academic excellence, batch sizes are capped. Once you submit the enquiry form or message us via WhatsApp, our team will share the upcoming intake schedule, syllabus roadmap, and orientation details corresponding to your chosen grade or competitive exam target.
