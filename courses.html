Organic Chemistry Specialization

Classes 11 & 12, NEET, JEE & Foundation Learners

Mentored directly by Aarushi Kasturiwal, M.Sc. in Organic Chemistry. Learn to see Organic Chemistry as an interconnected network of electron flows, stability drivers, and mechanisms rather than endless isolated reactions.

What is Covered:
General Organic Chemistry (GOC) Electronic Effects: Inductive, Mesomeric, Hyperconjugation Aromaticity & Anti-Aromaticity (Hückel's Rule) Optical & Geometrical Isomerism (Stereochemistry) Electrophilic & Nucleophilic Substitutions (SN1, SN2) Elimination Mechanisms (E1, E2, E1cB) Aldol, Cannizzaro & Named Organic Condensations Reagent Chemistry & Multi-Step Roadmaps
Delivery Mode: Mechanism-first masterclasses + reaction mapping drills
Duration & Schedule: Announced per academic intake batch

Hands-On Science

Where Theory Connects With Real Laboratory Chemistry

Chemistry is an experimental science. At COC, theoretical mechanisms are systematically linked with actual laboratory procedures—from qualitative salt analysis and acid-base titrations to organic functional group tests.

Students do not merely memorize textbook results; they learn to observe colour changes, precipitate formation, and gas evolutions, transforming abstract equations into permanent conceptual understanding.
