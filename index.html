Welcome to Chamber of Chemistry

Understand Chemistry. Build Confidence. Achieve More.

“Chamber of Chemistry is an educational initiative created with the belief that quality learning should be accessible to every student.”

Our aim is to make Chemistry engaging, understandable and connected to real learning needs. Through concept-based teaching, structured study material and regular practice, we seek to help students strengthen their fundamentals and approach examinations with confidence.

At COC, we believe that every difficult topic becomes manageable when it is explained with clarity, patience and the right learning approach. We encourage curiosity, independent thinking and consistent effort.

Meet the Mind Behind Chamber of Chemistry

Knowledge, Research and a Passion for Teaching.

Chamber of Chemistry was founded by Aarushi Kasturiwal, a Chemistry educator with a strong academic background and a passion for teaching. She holds an M.Sc. in Organic Chemistry and is a gold medallist. She has also completed a B.Ed., an M.A. in Education, and is currently pursuing a Ph.D. as a research scholar.

Her academic and research interests include Organic Chemistry, spectroscopy and the study of natural products as green corrosion inhibitors. Her research work involves the use of spectroscopic characterisation techniques such as ¹H NMR, UV–Visible spectroscopy and FTIR to investigate chemical compounds and their interactions.

Academic Trust

Our Learning Philosophy

Why true achievement in Chemistry stems from conceptual clarity rather than mechanical rote memorisation.

❝

Chemistry is neither an arbitrary collection of disconnected formulas nor a catalog of reactions to be learned by rote. It is the molecular language of the physical universe, governed by elegant thermodynamic principles and electron behaviours.

At Chamber of Chemistry, our pledge to students and parents is built on academic honesty and pedagogical rigour. We do not make exaggerated commercial claims. Instead, we invest our energy in providing crystal-clear explanations, rigorous NCERT foundation teaching, and a respectful learning environment where students cultivate genuine scientific curiosity and examination confidence.

Aarushi Kasturiwal • Founder & Educator, Chamber of Chemistry
