Our Identity & Pedagogy

About Chamber of Chemistry

Dedicated to making Chemistry engaging, understandable and connected to real student learning needs.

Educational Initiative

“Chamber of Chemistry was established on the firm belief that every difficult topic becomes manageable when it is explained with clarity, patience and the right learning approach.”

Through concept-based teaching, structured study material and regular practice, we seek to help students strengthen their fundamentals and approach examinations with genuine confidence.

We encourage curiosity, independent thinking and consistent effort, fostering a supportive environment where asking questions is celebrated and scientific reasoning takes precedence over rote memorisation.

👁️

Our Vision

To build a trusted and accessible Chemistry learning platform that empowers students to understand the subject deeply, perform confidently in academic and competitive examinations, and develop a lasting interest in science.

🎯

Our Mission

  • ✔ Make Chemistry concepts clear, logical and approachable.
  • ✔ Provide structured learning support for school and competitive-examination students.
  • ✔ Encourage conceptual understanding instead of rote memorisation.
  • ✔ Offer useful learning resources and opportunities for regular practice.
  • ✔ Create a positive learning environment where students feel supported and motivated.
Pedagogical Framework

Our Teaching Values

Every lecture, study sheet and problem set produced by Chamber of Chemistry is guided by five foundational principles:

💡

Clarity

Explain the 'why' behind every concept. From orbital overlap to equilibrium shifts, students learn the root cause of every phenomenon.

🔄

Consistency

Encourage regular study, revision and disciplined problem practice to build lasting long-term academic memory.

🌍

Accessibility

Make quality educational support available to a wider student community through open learning initiatives.

⚖️

Integrity

Communicate course details, syllabus expectations, results and student feedback honestly and without exaggeration.

🔬

Curiosity

Connect textbook Chemistry with laboratory methods and fascinating chemical reactions occurring in everyday life.

Founder Profile

Aarushi Kasturiwal

Knowledge, Research and a Passion for Teaching.

Chamber of Chemistry was founded by Aarushi Kasturiwal, a Chemistry educator with a strong academic background and a passion for teaching. She holds an M.Sc. in Organic Chemistry and is a gold medallist. She has also completed a Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.), an M.A. in Education, and is currently pursuing doctoral research as a Ph.D. research scholar.

Her academic and research interests include Organic Chemistry, spectroscopy and the study of natural products as green corrosion inhibitors. Her research work involves the use of spectroscopic characterisation techniques such as ¹H NMR (Proton Nuclear Magnetic Resonance), UV–Visible spectroscopy and FTIR (Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy) to investigate chemical compounds and their interactions.

Alongside her academic journey, she has extensive teaching and educational administration experience across Dehradun since 2021, with an abiding interest in making Chemistry easier for students through clear explanations, conceptual learning and structured preparation. Through Chamber of Chemistry, she brings together academic understanding, effective teaching and accessible learning resources.
